DESIGN OPPORTUNITY #1
HMW create a centralized 
hub that automatically 
logs data so coaches 
don’t waste time on 
manual entry?
BRIDGING FRAGMENTED DATA
Coaches want to focus on training, recruiting, and 
player growth, but context switching between tools 
to log texts, calls, and compliance data all day pulls 
them away from the job they love.
Many different tools exist to satisfy compliance requirements
Excel is still heavily used to bridge gaps
DESIGN OPPORTUNITY #2
HMW make a staff's 
“intuitive” recruiting 
criteria visible instead of 
locked in the coach's 
head?
 Making the human side of recruiting visible
Every coaching staff recruits to its own model, but 
that model lives in people’ s heads. It’ s easy to 
quantify what matters in the game but harder to 
capture the team fit (dynamics, synergy, and values) 
and it rarely gets shared in documentation. 
A player with a good attitude and sportsmanship can 
make all the difference - but is hard to quantify

DESIGN OPPORTUNITY #4
HMW help coaches make 
balanced roster decisions 
across high school and 
transfer recruits?
THE ROSTER DILEMMA
High school recruits offer long-term potential, while 
transfer players provide proven, immediate impact. 
Because they are evaluated using completely 
different types of evidence, it is difficult for coaches 
to consistently weigh the trade-offs when building a 
roster
The NCAA Transfer Portal has become a major factor in collegiate 
soccer recruiting and roster management.
W e are not attempting to automate 
or quantify human relationships. 
Culture, trust, and interpersonal fit 
remain essential coaching 
judgments.
Compliance 
requirements are set 
in stone, so our job 
is to work around 
them.
